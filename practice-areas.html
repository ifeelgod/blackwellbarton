Strategic Regulatory Roadmaps

NDA/BLA Submission Architecture & Planning

AGENCY ENGAGEMENT

Health Authority Meeting Mastery

We have managed 180+ successful interactions with FDA, EMA, PMDA, and Health Canada. Every meeting is an opportunity to shape your regulatory narrative.

FDA Type A, B, C Meetings

From resolving clinical holds (Type A) to critical milestone alignments (Type B) and specialized guidance (Type C). We draft precise briefing documents that constrain agency responses to actionable feedback, avoiding open-ended queries that invite scope creep.

EMA Scientific Advice & INTERACT

Securing early alignment with CHMP and national competent authorities. For early-stage biologicals and ATMPs, our INTERACT meeting preparation provides vital de-risking before formal IND/CTA phases begin.

Pre-BLA/Pre-NDA Meetings

The ultimate submission stress-test. We architect Pre-NDA/BLA packages to secure agreement on formatting, dataset structure, and facility readiness, minimizing Day 74 filing issues and Refusal to File (RTF) risks.

Advisory Committee (AdCom) Preparation

High-stakes defense. We develop core presentations, conduct grueling Q&A stress tests, and assemble briefing materials that distill complex risk-benefit profiles into undeniable clinical imperatives.

EXPEDITED PATHWAYS

Expedited Programs & Special Designations

Maximize your eligibility for accelerated regulatory pathways. We've secured 30+ expedited designations across breakthrough therapy, fast track, PRIME, and RMAT.

BTD

FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation

  • ✓ Intensive FDA guidance
  • ✓ Rolling review eligible

Our Approach: We pinpoint the preliminary clinical evidence that undeniably demonstrates substantial improvement over existing therapies.

FTD

FDA Fast Track Designation

  • ✓ More frequent meetings
  • ✓ Priority review potential

Our Approach: We build the narrative around the unmet medical need and how the nonclinical or clinical data addresses it robustly.

PRM

EMA PRIME (Priority Medicines)

  • ✓ Enhanced EMA support
  • ✓ Accelerated assessment

Our Approach: European-tailored messaging that highlights major therapeutic advantage in areas of unmet need within the EU landscape.

RMA

FDA RMAT Designation

  • ✓ All Fast Track/BTD benefits
  • ✓ Early CMC discussions

Our Approach: We specialize in the unique CMC, comparability, and durable efficacy narratives required for cell and gene therapies.
